THE Illusionists BY GRAHAM EDWARDS It’s been an incredible 40 years since Cinefex was first published. In those four dec - ades, the visual effects industry has changed out of all recognition. Or has it? Tools and techniques have certainly evolved, but have the goals of the cinematic illusionist evolved along with them? How does the past inform the present, and what future lies ahead? To chew over these meaty topics, we invited a group of seasoned visual effects professionals to join us in a free - flowing roundtable discussion. First we would indulge in a little nostalgia for the good old days, before opening things up to explore the ways in which effects techniques have changed over time, discuss current trends and philosophies, and speculate on all those wonders that are yet to come. We had no problem coming up with a list of people we wanted to contact. But our hearts quailed when that list of potential interviewees began to climb into the hundreds. How could we possibly keep a lid on the numbers? At last a solution presented itself. We would take as our starting point films that have won the Academy Award for Best Visual Effects — be ginning in 1980, the year Cinefex was first published — and interview a tidy total of 21 key visual effects supervisors from the teams involved. Our heartfelt thanks to all those who came to the party, and our humble apologies to the many hundreds we just couldn’t fit around the table! 1 / 24
